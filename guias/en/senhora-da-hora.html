How much does it cost to rent in Senhora da Hora?

The median rent in Senhora da Hora is €11.11/m² (INE 2024). A typical 2-bedroom apartment (T2 in Portuguese) of around 70 m² costs approximately €778/month. Rents in Senhora da Hora (€11.11/m²) are slightly above the Portuguese national average of €10.50/m².

Matosinhos recorded 39.7 crimes per 1,000 inhabitants in 2023 (INE), giving Senhora da Hora a safety score of 51/100. Matosinhos recorded 39.7 crimes per 1,000 inhabitants (INE 2023), below the national average of ~35.
